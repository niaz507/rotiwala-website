The Story

The bustling street stalls of Dhaka, right here on Kingsway.

Introducing authentic Bangladeshi cuisine to Vancouver. From chotpoti to the aromatic, layered biryani prepared with love and precision, our offerings tell a story of tradition and capture the essence of Bangladesh’s rich heritage & food culture.

রোটিওয়ালা — স্বাদে বাংলাদেশ

01

Street Food Favorites

Experience the bustling street stalls of Dhaka with our fuchka (crispy pani puri) and chotpoti (tangy chickpea and potato chaat). These bite-sized delights burst with spicy, savory, and sweet notes – an authentic Bangladeshi craze that’s hard to resist!

02

Aromatic Biryani

Savor our signature biryani, a fragrant rice dish slow-cooked with delicate spices and tender meat (try the traditional Beef Tehari). Each mouthful is a journey through exotic flavors, true to its Old Dhaka heritage.

03

Fresh Daily Roti

Enjoy soft, pillowy roti straight from our tawa (griddle) – made fresh every morning. Perfect for scooping up curry or as a snack on their own, in whole wheat and classic mix flour varieties, baked daily for maximum freshness.

04

Savory Snacks & Sweets

From golden samosas and singara to mughlai paratha, we have all your teatime favorites. Don’t miss our kalojam, misti doi and silky egg pudding – a little sweet treat to round out your meal.

05

Coffee & Chai Delights

Whether you crave a cup of Bangladeshi-style cha or a sweet lassi, pair your brew with a hot snack and enjoy a cozy, relaxed vibe that’s true to South Asian café culture.
